## Patient and sample details

62-year-old patient with an advanced solid tumour. FFPE tissue from a recent biopsy of a progressing metastatic lesion was submitted for molecular profiling. Estimated tumour content: 70%. Patient and sample identifiers are listed in the report header.

## History / Reason for testing

The patient has progressive metastatic disease following previous systemic treatment. A new biopsy was obtained to characterize the current molecular profile and support multidisciplinary discussion of further investigations and potential treatment options.

## Interpretation summary

Selected sequence variants include findings in TP53, RB1 and PTEN, together with additional variants listed in the results table. Copy-number findings include pronounced increases involving MYCN and REL. RNA findings include a TMPRSS2–ERG fusion candidate and a MET splice candidate. TMB is 17.1 mut/Mb, the MSI score is 4.13, and the HRD score is 42. The selected findings are presented for multidisciplinary review.

## Key relevant findings / therapeutic context

The key relevant findings bring together selected DNA, copy-number and RNA results. Their potential relevance should be assessed alongside tumour type, histology, previous treatment and the final variant classification. A treatment decision has not been assigned in this report; the findings provide the basis for further discussion at the molecular tumour board.

## Variant description

The selected small variants include TP53 G245D, RB1 R445X and PTEN T319X. CHEK2 T410fs and HNF1A P289fs are recorded as VUS in the current review. The results table provides the individual allele frequencies and review classifications. Copy-number and RNA findings are reported separately, with their corresponding quality assessments.
